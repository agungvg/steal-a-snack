Steal a Snack — a 3D Roblox brainrot game. Collect absurd snack-born characters that earn money every second, keep them on your base, climb the rarity ladder, mutate and rebirth them — while a Hunger Wave sweeps the map and rival players try to steal your catch.

## Run it

```sh
rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl
```

Then open game.rbxl in Roblox Studio and press Play.
